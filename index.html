About Us

We are the EduQuest Academy team. Phonics World is our companion site for young learners (ages 4–10) working through the 5-level phonics journey — letters, sounds, and words, turned into fun interactive lessons and games.

We believe technology should help our kids learn, not just keep them occupied.
